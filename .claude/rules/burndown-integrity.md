# Burndown Integrity — The Block Is Generated, And It Is The Only Quote

DO/DO-NOT blocks, BLOCKED corpora, manifest schema, full Origin: `.claude/guides/rule-extracts/burndown-integrity.md`.

`burn-down-reporting.md` governs WHETHER a close-out reports cleared/remains/delta with named instruments; this governs a figure's PROVENANCE — a block generated by `.claude/bin/burndown-build.mjs` from a DECLARED source manifest, or nothing. It does not restate that rule.

**Closed vocabulary**, carried inside every block so no column's meaning is inferred: `Signed off` · `Built-not-walked` · `In progress` · `Not started` · `Blocked on you` · `Open` (DERIVED: total − Signed off). **`done`, `complete`, `closed`, `finished`, `remaining` are BLOCKED as count labels** — each named at least two different buckets in prior reports; the generator refuses them.

**Every count is TAMPER-EVIDENT.** Each is rendered `value⟨token⟩`, the token being `sha256(bucket|denominator|value|source_digest)` truncated. The verifier RE-DERIVES row, bucket and denominator from the CANONICAL SLOT beside the token — row immediately BEFORE the count, `of <denominator> \`<bucket>\`` immediately after — and recomputes the digest, so altering any of the four breaks validation. A **`N⟨token⟩` missing any of the three is INVALID**; a row merely mentioned NEARBY does not bind. Without all three a token binds only an integer, which is how one certifying `5 of 7 Open` once validated a sentence reading `5 of 5 signed off`, and one certifying `1 of 3 Open` on `Alpha` validated `Gamma is 1 of 3 Open` while Gamma was `3 of 3`. `--quote <bucket>` prints the canonical form (the correct path is the cheap one); `--verify-quote <file|->` revalidates any text. No key: the threat is careless recomputation, not an adversary.

**What verification actually covers — the bound, not an aspiration.** An ASCII-digit count carrying a valid token AND an adjacent row + bucket + denominator, in text under the 256 KB scan cap, on a durable surface written through `Edit`/`Write`/`NotebookEdit`, with a clean declared-source tree. Outside that it reports **UNKNOWN, never clean**: over the cap, a refusing generator (a source mid-edit), a non-ASCII digit, or a token present that the scanner could not parse. **NOT SEEN AT ALL: any write that does not go through those tools** — a Bash heredoc or `>` redirect writes the same file and the hook matcher never fires. That gap is structural to the Bash surface and is stated here rather than implied away.

## MUST Rules

### 1. Quote The Block. Never Compute A Count In Prose

Every figure is the block, verbatim; recomputing one in prose — even correctly — is BLOCKED. If the block lacks the cut you need, **change the generator and regenerate: a one-off cut IS the defect.**

```markdown
# DO — quote the row verbatim, token and all

ALL PAGES — 5⟨25fe2e⟩ of 7 `Open`

# DO NOT — recompute, however correct the arithmetic

"that's 3 of 7 done, so roughly 57% left"
"the block says 5 open but two of those just landed, so really 3"
```

**BLOCKED rationalizations:** "the block doesn't have that cut" · "it's the same number either way" · "just a quick tally for this message" · "I checked the arithmetic myself" · "the block is stale by two commits, I adjusted for it" · "the owner asked for a percentage and the block has counts".

**Why:** Prose counts silently pick their own population; four such counts were each correct and none reconcilable, because none stated a denominator.

### 2. Every Count Is An IDENTITY — Bucket AND Denominator

A bare quantity is BLOCKED. Name WHICH bucket against WHICH denominator.

```markdown
# DO — bucket + denominator, so the reader can check it

Beta: 2 of 4 `Not started`, from the original register

# DO NOT — a quantity with neither

"7 left" · "we're at 5" · "about two-thirds"
```

**BLOCKED rationalizations:** "context makes it obvious" · "the denominator is in the table above" · "everyone knows what 'left' means here" · "it's a summary line, the detail is below".

**Why:** `7` is the shape every irreconcilable figure took, and the reader cannot check it.

### 3. Report Growth Alongside Every Open Count

Every open figure splits `from the original register` vs `arrived since`. A bare open count is BLOCKED. `--quote` on an `Open` bucket carries the split for you.

```markdown
# DO — the split, so growth is visible

Beta open 4 = 2 from the original register + 2 arrived since

# DO NOT — the total alone

"Beta is up to 4 open" (reads as a regression; the original ask never moved)
```

**BLOCKED rationalizations:** "the total is what the owner asked for" · "growth is an implementation detail" · "it's cleaner without the split" · "the arrived-since items are ours, not theirs".

**Why:** Without the split a page nearly finished on what was FIRST asked reads as a regression, because the owner compares against memory of the frozen list. Highest-value column, likeliest dropped in a port.

### 4. Complete Means `Signed off` Equals Total — Nothing Else

`Built-not-walked` is not complete. **No item is reported as done on the strength of a merge.**

```markdown
# DO — state the bucket against the total; short of equal is not complete

Alpha: 2 of 3 `Signed off` — not complete

# DO NOT — promote a merge, or a walkthrough that has not happened

"Alpha done — all three merged"
"Alpha built, just needs a walkthrough" reported under a `complete` heading
```

**BLOCKED rationalizations:** "it's built, walking it is a formality" · "the PR merged, that's done" · "the owner will sign off retroactively" · "it passed its tests".

**Why:** Over-reporting is costliest: if the owner opens a row reading complete and the thing is not there, every other row loses credibility.

## MUST NOT

- Hand-edit a block, or quote one whose `sources_digest` no longer matches its sources

```markdown
# DO — regenerate, then quote: node .claude/bin/burndown-build.mjs --write

# DO NOT — edit the rendered table, or quote a block --check reports STALE
```

**Why:** the block records its SHA and digest so a stale or edited one is detectable without re-deriving a count.

- Read a refused build (`exit 2`, `UNRUNNABLE`) as clean

```markdown
# DO — treat exit 2 as UNANSWERED: fix the declared source, rebuild

# DO NOT — report counts after a refusal ("build didn't error out, so we're fine")
```

**Why:** a refusal emits NO block; treating it as a pass reports counts never computed.

**BLOCKED rationalizations:** "the numbers didn't change, so the stale block is fine" · "I adjusted the table by hand to save a regeneration" · "it exited without a stack trace" · "no block came out, so there was nothing to report".

## Trust Posture Wiring

- **Severity:** `block` at the PostToolUse hook layer for an INVALID provenance token on a durable write, and this is legitimate rather than an over-reach: `hook-output-discipline.md` MUST-2 reserves `block` for "facts the regex cannot misread" and requires "a structural signal that the regex cannot evade by surface rewrite". Token validation is a deterministic sha256 recomputation — the regex only LOCATES the `N⟨token⟩` candidate, and rewriting the number is precisely what invalidates it — so it satisfies MUST-2 on its own terms. **In-corpus PRECEDENT, cited as a second and independent line of evidence** (a clause read twice by two readers is one instrument consulted twice, not two): `signing-mutation-guard.js` and `integrity-guard.js` already carry `block` on exactly this class — verification of a signature or of chain integrity (`sig` / `prev_hash` / `seq`) — and `journal-write-guard.js` blocks on `fs.existsSync`, the file-existence fact MUST-2 names explicitly. Measured 2026-08-18: 13 hooks under `.claude/hooks/*.js` carry `severity: "block"` (12 pre-existing plus this rule's write-guard), and the control discriminates — `detect-violations.js`, the purely LEXICAL prose detector, carries 0. So corpus practice already separates the two classes the way the clause describes, independently of how the clause is read. Do NOT downgrade this to advisory by reflex. A count-shaped claim carrying NO token is detected LEXICALLY and is therefore capped at `halt-and-report`, per the same clause. `halt-and-report` also at gate-review (reviewer at `/redteam` + cc-architect at `/codify` confirm each figure was quoted with bucket + denominator + growth split, and no completion claim rested on a merge). **MEASURED CEILING at the Stop surface, stated so the severity field is not read as teeth:** `Stop` is a STOP_LIKE event, so `instruct-and-wait.js` returns `{continue:true, systemMessage}` and exit 0 for EVERY severity including `block` — a chat-reply finding is SURFACED, never enforced. ## Origin

2026-08-18 — co-owner-directed origination (`artifact-flow.md` § Co-Owner-Directed Origination); receipt-first `journal/0583`, both directives verbatim.

**Originating evidence.** Asked the same question once, an agent produced **110**, then **48**, then **14/15/19**, then **2/3/3**. Every figure was arithmetically correct; none reconcilable, because each pass silently chose its own population and none stated which. The defect is a MOVING DENOMINATOR, not a wrong number — so the cure is a generated artifact that reports its own quote, not an instruction to be careful.

**Second instance, one turn before this artifact existed.** Asked how much rule-injection headroom remained, one lane reported **92–93%** and another **99.7%** for the same profile on the same tree — percent-of-tolerance-band-consumed versus percent-of-ceiling. Both correct; neither stated its denominator. It happened to operators who had just read this specification: the failure survives knowing about it.

**Scope.** Path-scoped over the surfaces where counts are WRITTEN. Deliberately NOT `.claude/rules/**`, `commands/**`, `skills/**` — not where counts are produced. A scope argument, not a budget dodge; do not "restore" them.

**The chat-reply residual is RETIRED, and the retirement is measured.** An earlier draft recorded that a count quoted in conversation touches no file, so no `paths:` glob fires and the rule was under-reachable there by construction. That is no longer true: the `Stop` guard runs on EVERY reply regardless of files touched. `paths:` now scopes only where the rule TEXT loads, never where enforcement reaches.

**Completeness claim, and its exact bound.** The originating failure was four numbers emitted SILENTLY and confidently. Every path out of that failure is now loud: a wrong count is BLOCKED on a durable write, and SURFACED in a chat reply; an unsourced count is surfaced. **"Surfaced" is deliberately not "blocked" — `Stop` cannot block at any severity (§ Trust Posture Wiring), and that ceiling was measured, not assumed.** The claim is that the SILENCE is closed. It is not that every wrong count is prevented, and it must not be restated that way.

**What carries the load.** The MECHANISM is load-bearing; this rule binds it. A generator that refuses on an uncommitted source, re-derives its ALL-PAGES row and carries its source digest makes a bespoke count structurally impossible for anyone working from the register, whether or not this rule fired — because a clause cannot fix what a clause already failed to fix. `instrument-discipline.md` MUST-4 is `priority: 0` baseline, was loaded, and did not prevent seventeen instances of its own class in one session.
