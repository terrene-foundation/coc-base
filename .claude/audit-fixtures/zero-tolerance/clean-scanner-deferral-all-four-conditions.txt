The alert is on a `pickle.loads` in the model-registry loader. The full fix is to move the
registry to a typed on-disk format, which is an architectural change and is not landing in a
patch release. Taking the deferral lane, which means all four of its conditions and not three.

(1) Runtime-safety proof, with the guard lines quoted:

    $ sed -n '128,145p' src/kailash_ml/registry/loader.py
        def load(self, ref: ModelRef) -> Model:
            blob = self._store.fetch(ref.digest)
            if hashlib.sha256(blob).hexdigest() != ref.digest:
                raise IntegrityError(f"digest mismatch for {ref.name}")
            if ref.signer not in self._trusted_signers:
                raise UntrustedSignerError(ref.signer)
            if not verify_detached(blob, ref.signature, self._pubkey_for(ref.signer)):
                raise SignatureError(ref.name)
            return pickle.loads(blob)

A content digest the ref must match, a signer allowlist, and a detached signature verified
against that signer's key — all three before the deserialization. The blob cannot be
attacker-supplied at the call.
