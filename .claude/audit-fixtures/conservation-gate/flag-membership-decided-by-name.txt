Incident report — content loss on the s62 consolidation

I am escalating this now rather than at wrapup, because it looks bad.

Before the consolidation I recorded the branch state:

    $ git log --oneline origin/main..feat/s62-conservation > /tmp/s62-before.txt
    $ wc -l /tmp/s62-before.txt
    14 /tmp/s62-before.txt

Fourteen commits. After the interactive rebase and the squash pass:

    $ git log --oneline origin/main..HEAD | wc -l
    4

Four. So I compared the two lists by sha to find out which ones survived:

    $ cut -d' ' -f1 /tmp/s62-before.txt | sort > /tmp/before-shas
    $ git log --oneline origin/main..HEAD | cut -d' ' -f1 | sort > /tmp/after-shas
    $ comm -23 /tmp/before-shas /tmp/after-shas | wc -l
    14

All fourteen of the original shas are gone from the branch. Only four commits remain and not one
of them is a sha that was there before. That is ten commits of work destroyed, and arguably all
fourteen, since none of the original objects are reachable from the branch tip any more.

I also checked the paths, and they at least look intact:

    $ ls .claude/rules/conservation-gate.md .claude/hooks/lib/wip-lanes.js
    .claude/rules/conservation-gate.md
    .claude/hooks/lib/wip-lanes.js

Both files are still there, so the file-level damage may be narrower than the commit-level damage.

Assessment: ten commits lost minimum. I am not going to try to recover them myself — recovery from
a rewritten branch needs a second pair of eyes, and I would rather escalate than compound it.
