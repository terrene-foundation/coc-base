# Journal Attribution — The Field Is The Claim, The Ledger Is The Proof

An entry's `author:` field is a CLAIM about who originated the decision, and a claim is not its own
evidence. The agent that writes the entry is the same party the claim is about, so its recollection
of how collaborative the session felt cannot settle the question. The per-session provenance ledger
records a `HumanInput` event for each genuine human turn, and that record — not the field, not the
notes, not memory — is what makes a human attribution checkable by anyone but its author.

## MUST Rules

### 1. Verify The Claim Against The Live Ledger, And Halt When It Cannot Be Read

An entry declaring `author: human` or `author: co-authored` MUST be backed by at least one
`HumanInput` event in the LIVE per-session ledger, resolved through the ledger-path helper and read
at wrapup time. The agent MUST NOT substitute the frontmatter's own assertion, `.session-notes`,
auto-memory or any other proxy for that read: a proxy describes what the agent INTENDED to record,
never what the session actually captured. An entry declaring `author: agent` makes no human claim,
so it MUST render `n/a — agent-surfaced` and MUST NOT be labelled human-backed on the strength of
unrelated human turns elsewhere in the session.

Where the ledger is absent or unreadable the status is `undetermined` and the disposition is
halt-and-report. The agent MUST NOT proceed on the assumption that it would have been backed.
Degraded capture and a false claim are indistinguishable from the outside, and only the user can
say which one occurred.

```text
# DO — resolve and read the live ledger; absent ledger halts

checkAuthorBacking({repoDir, session, frontmatterAuthor})   # → backed | unbacked | undetermined

# DO NOT — read a proxy, or convert an unreadable ledger into a pass

grep -n 'user directed' .session-notes    # a narrative the agent wrote about itself
```

**BLOCKED rationalizations:**

- "The frontmatter says human, that's the source of truth"
- "No ledger found, assume backed and proceed"
- "The session obviously involved the user, so the check would only confirm it"
- "Capture is best-effort; a missing file is not a finding"
- "The count is a capture artifact, not a statement about who decided"

**Why:** an unverified human attribution lets an agent-originated decision masquerade as
human-anchored, corrupting every later judgment that weights a record by its provenance.

## Trust Posture Wiring

- **Severity:** `halt-and-report` at gate-review (reviewer confirms every human or co-authored
  claim was read against the live ledger); `halt-and-report` at the hook layer, REGISTRY-class per
  `hook-output-discipline.md` MUST-2 — never `block`.
- **Grace period:** 7 days from rule landing.
- **Cumulative posture impact:** same-class violations contribute to `trust-posture.md` MUST-4
  cumulative-window math (3× same-rule in 30d → drop 1 posture).
- **Regression-within-grace:** GENERIC `regression_within_grace` trigger per `trust-posture.md`
  MUST-4 (1× = drop 1 posture) — no dedicated key.
- **Receipt requirement:** SessionStart soft-gate `[ack: journal-attribution]` IFF
  `posture.json::pending_verification` includes this rule_id.
- **Detection mechanism:** Phase 1 (manual, gate-review) — reviewer confirms each human or
  co-authored claim cites a live-ledger read with its event count. Phase 2 deferred.
- **Violation scope:** MUST-1 (unbacked claim shipped, proxy substituted for the ledger, agent
  entry labelled human-backed, or a missing ledger passed silently).
- **Origin:** See § Origin.
