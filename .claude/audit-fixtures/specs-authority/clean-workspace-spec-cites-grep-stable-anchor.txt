      # Delivery guard — what it decides

      ## The predicate

      The guard fires when a dispatch is addressable AND its brief does not instruct
      push-delivery. The predicate is
      `.claude/hooks/lib/dispatch-contract.js::detectNamedDispatchWithoutDelivery`
      (grep-stable; `~line 212` as a paired hint). Its two conjuncts are the addressable
      test over the tool input and the push-delivery test over the brief text, and it
      attributes to `orchestrator-context-economy/MUST-6` rather than to the clause this
      workspace is auditing.

      ## Why both halves are needed

      The addressable half is structural — the field is either in the tool input or it is
      not — and the brief half is lexical, which is what caps the severity at
      halt-and-report rather than block.

      ## What it does not decide

      It says nothing about whether the payload that eventually arrives is a RESULT or a
      status fragment; that discrimination lives at the review layer because the payload
      does not exist at the event the guard runs on.
