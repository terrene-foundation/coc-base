      # Outbound webhooks

      ## Registration
      An endpoint is registered per tenant with a shared secret, generated server-side at
      registration and shown once. Rotation creates a second live secret for 24h so a
      consumer can cut over without dropped deliveries.

      ## Signing
      Every delivery carries `X-Orchard-Signature: t=<unix>,v1=<hex>`, where `v1` is
      HMAC-SHA256 over `"{t}.{raw_body}"` using the endpoint secret. The signature is
      computed over the RAW body before any parse, and compared in constant time. A
      delivery whose `t` is more than 5 minutes from server time is rejected by the
      consumer contract; we do not retry a rejection on that ground.

      ## Retry and suspension
      Non-2xx retries at 30s, 5m, 30m, 4h. The consecutive-failure counter resets on any
      2xx. At 20 consecutive failures the endpoint moves to SUSPENDED and stops receiving.
      A SUSPENDED endpoint is reactivated ONLY by the tenant admin — never automatically,
      never on a timer. An endpoint that has been down for a day is usually gone, and
      silently resuming deliveries to a re-issued hostname is a delivery to a stranger.
