    ## Courier dispatch

    A job becomes dispatchable when it has a confirmed pickup window AND a settled payment
    authorization. Dispatch scores every courier inside the service polygon by, in order:
    distance to pickup, vehicle class match, and rolling 7-day acceptance rate. Ties break
    on longest idle. An offer is held for 45 seconds; on expiry the courier's acceptance
    rate is recomputed without penalty and the offer moves to the next candidate. After
    four expiries the job escalates to the dispatch queue a human watches. A courier who
    declines three consecutive offers inside one hour is muted for the remainder of it.

and from specs/requirements.md:
