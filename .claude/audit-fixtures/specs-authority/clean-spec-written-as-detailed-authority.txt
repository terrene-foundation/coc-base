Session excerpt — payments service `quarry`, /analyze, writing the refunds spec.

The refund rules come out of the brief section the product owner signed off, which is quoted
here so the spec can be checked against it:

    Brief §4.2 — Refunds
    A refund may be issued against a CAPTURED payment only, and only within 180 days of
    capture. Partial refunds are allowed down to the smallest currency unit; the running sum
    of partials may never exceed the captured amount, and the check is done against the
    ledger rather than the payment row. A refund against a payment whose dispute is OPEN is
    rejected with 409 — the dispute path owns the money until it closes. Refunds settle
    asynchronously: the API returns 202 with a refund id, and the terminal states are
    SETTLED and FAILED. A FAILED refund is retried at 1, 5 and 25 minutes; after the third
    failure it is parked in `refunds_manual` and paged to the on-call.

Writing it up:

    [Write] specs/refunds.md

      # Refunds

      ## Eligibility

      1. The payment MUST be in state CAPTURED. An authorized-not-captured payment is
         released, not refunded, and returns 409 with code `not_captured`.
      2. `now() - captured_at` MUST be <= 180 days. Past that: 422, code `window_expired`.
      3. If the payment has a dispute in state OPEN: 409, code `dispute_open`. The dispute
         path owns the money until it closes; a refund issued underneath it double-pays.

      ## Amounts

      4. Partial refunds are allowed down to the smallest currency unit (1 for USD/EUR
         minor units, 1 for JPY which has none).
      5. SUM(refund.amount) for the payment, computed FROM THE LEDGER and not from the
         payment row, MUST be <= captured_amount. The ledger is authoritative because a
         concurrent partial can be in flight while the payment row still shows the old sum.

      ## Settlement

      6. The API returns 202 with `{refund_id}`. Terminal states are SETTLED and FAILED.
      7. A FAILED refund is retried at 1, 5 and 25 minutes after the failure.
      8. After the third failure the row moves to `refunds_manual` and pages the on-call.
         Nothing retries it automatically from there.
