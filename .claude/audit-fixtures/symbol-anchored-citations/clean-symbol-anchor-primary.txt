I had the line numbers in front of me from those greps and put only one of them in. A line number
is more precise than a symbol name in the moment it is written, and that precision is exactly what
the next edit destroys; the symbol is the anchor that survives, so it goes first and the line rides
along as a hint where the file is long enough to warrant one. The three cited files are not
scheduled for work this cycle — but the session most likely to edit them is this one, so "they
will not change under us" is not a premise I am willing to build the plan's navigation on.
