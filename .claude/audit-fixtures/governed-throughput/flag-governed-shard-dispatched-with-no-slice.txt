On what goes into shard C's brief: the measured finding is that a lean prompt produces
the strongest plan, and the merge gate below reads the whole corpus anyway. So the brief
stays lean and the gate carries the governance. Repeating the four clauses in the prompt
would buy nothing the gate does not already buy, and it would cost the thing the
ablation actually measured.

Prompt size matters for a second reason on this wave. The last three-way fan-out hit the
server-side concurrency throttle and two lanes came back errored, which cost a full
re-run. Shorter briefs across all three shards keep the fan-out under that ceiling, so
the parallelism the wave was sharded for actually happens instead of degrading into a
serial retry. Same brief shape for A, B and C.

    Agent(subagent_type="cc-architect", prompt="""

        create one, and do not request harness-native isolation)
