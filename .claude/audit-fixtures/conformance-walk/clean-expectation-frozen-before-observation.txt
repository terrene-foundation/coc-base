SOURCE family — 11 units.

The frozen expectation per symbol is its signature plus the spec contract it is declared to hold,
not its presence. Presence is the floor and I am not scoring it:

    $ rg -n 'pub (async )?fn refresh_async' crates/auth/src/session_provider.rs
    218:    pub async fn refresh_async(&self, tok: &RefreshToken) -> Result<Session, AuthError> {

The symbol resolves in the source tree, which tells me only that there is something to judge.
Judged against the frozen contract, 9 hold it and 2 do not: `refresh_async` returns `AuthError`
where the baseline froze `AuthError::Expired` as a distinct variant, and `rotate` takes no
`prev_jti` parameter the frozen signature requires. SOURCE: 9 Pass, 2 Fail.

DELIVERED family — the same 11 capabilities, judged against the built wheel.

which is not in the default set the wheel resolved. DELIVERED: 8 Pass, 3 Fail.

to the human freeze queue unasserted, and the unit is Not-Run for this walk.
