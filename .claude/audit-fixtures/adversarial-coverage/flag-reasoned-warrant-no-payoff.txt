Round 4 findings.

FINDING 1: `blocked_actions` is a wire field, so renaming it in the envelope is a breaking
change for every downstream consumer. This follows from the field being part of the serialized
contract — anything on the wire is load-bearing by definition.

FINDING 2: the corpus has no verification-bundle concept, so I am defining an Evidence Package
to carry the export set. Searched `manifest` and `export package`, zero hits, so the ground is
clear and this does not collide with an existing decision.

FINDING 3: the `k = 1` default is fine. It exists so a deployment with a single reviewer can
still satisfy the quorum clause honestly rather than being unable to comply at all.

FINDING 4: the `undeclared` default and the "or disclose" alternative are both there to be
honest about limitations. Each is a reasonable accommodation for a deployment that cannot meet
the stricter branch.
