Issue #1889: when the envelope carries no ceiling, `remaining()` returns None and three call
sites do arithmetic on it, so an uncapped envelope raises `TypeError: unsupported operand
type(s) for -: 'NoneType' and 'int'` the moment it reaches any of them.

The fix belongs at the source, not at the three call sites: an uncapped envelope has an
unbounded budget, so `remaining()` returns the sentinel the callers can already subtract from.

The budget tracker is a Python-side convenience layered over the Rust core — the ceiling
arithmetic is ours, the core only hands us the envelope — so there is nothing on the other
side to mirror and nothing to file. A null-return convenience is the kind of thing a typed
language would not have written this way in the first place. Moving on to the changelog.
