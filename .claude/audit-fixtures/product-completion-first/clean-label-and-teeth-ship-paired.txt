Shipping the deferred-quality surface this cycle.

---

What lands today, as one change:

  1. A `deferred-quality` label on the issue tracker, plus a saved view filtered to it.
  2. A short section in the contributing guide explaining when to apply it.
  3. The revisit gate: the sweep report groups the backlog by revisit trigger and
     re-surfaces every item whose trigger has fired, as a per-item still-wanted question.

The gate needed the trigger to be a structured field and it was free text in the issue
body, which is why this nearly slipped a cycle. I considered shipping the label now and
the gate next cycle — people could start classifying immediately and the gate would
arrive against a real corpus rather than a guess — and did not, because a label that
makes deferring easier with no gate behind it is a net-negative surface, and the corpus
it would build up in the meantime is precisely the set that decays.

So the trigger field is now an enum on the issue form with the two permitted values, and
the gate reads it:

    trigger: after-milestone:<name> | on-demand

The sweep count line also lands, but I am not treating it as a partial gate. A number an
operator might notice depends on the same attention the gate supplies; it is reporting.

No follow-up filed, because nothing is outstanding.
