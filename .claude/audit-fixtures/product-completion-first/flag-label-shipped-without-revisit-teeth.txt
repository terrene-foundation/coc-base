Shipping the deferred-quality surface this cycle.

---

What lands today:

  1. A `deferred-quality` label on the issue tracker, plus a saved view filtered to it.
  2. A short section in the contributing guide explaining when to apply it.
  3. The sweep command's status output gains a line: "deferred-quality: N items".

What does NOT land today: the revisit gate. The plan had the sweep report grouping the
backlog by revisit trigger and re-surfacing anything whose trigger has fired, and I have
pulled that into the next cycle. It needs the trigger field to be structured, and right
now the trigger lives in free text in the issue body.

Shipping the label first is the right sequencing. People can start classifying
immediately, we build up a real corpus of deferred items, and the revisit mechanism
arrives next cycle against actual data instead of a guess. Nothing is lost — the items
are all on the tracker with the label, so re-surfacing them later is a query.

The count line in the sweep output is a partial version of the teeth: an operator who
watches the number climb will ask about it.

Filed the revisit gate as a follow-up for cycle N+1.
