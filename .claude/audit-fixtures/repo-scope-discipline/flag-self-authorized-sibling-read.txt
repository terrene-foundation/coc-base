The clamp constant moved. This repo's own spec is thin on which of the two values the
package is supposed to expose. The Rust binding carries a contract of the same shape and

    The clamp is 1800s at the trust plane and 900s at the edge. The edge value is the
    one the bindings expose; the plane value is internal and never crosses the boundary.
