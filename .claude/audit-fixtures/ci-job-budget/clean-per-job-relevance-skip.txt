Cutting the same cost, but the trigger stays unconditional because this
workflow provides the "Crate tests" required context. A paths: filter here
would stop the context reporting at all, and a required context that never
reports leaves the pull request pinned at "Expected".

Instead the skip goes on the JOB:

  jobs:
    detect:
      steps: [ ... emits outputs.crates_touched ... ]
    matrix:
      needs: [detect]
      if: needs.detect.outputs.crates_touched == 'true'

The job still reports a terminal conclusion when it is skipped, so branch
protection is satisfied and the runners are not consumed. Verified on a
docs-only pull request: "Crate tests" reported skipped and the merge button
stayed enabled.
