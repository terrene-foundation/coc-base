/**
 * @hook-event: PreToolUse:Bash (guard) — a CI-triggering `git push` is the one
 *   action whose cost depends on pool state, and PreToolUse:Bash is the boundary
 *   attempting it. The subject (the pending command, and the pool's CURRENT
 *   occupancy) exists at this instant and nowhere else.
 *
 * SEVERITY IS CAPPED AT halt-and-report. `triggersCi` consumes parseGitInvocations
 * (a PARSED signal, not lexical), so MUST-2 does not bite there — the cap holds on
 * three other grounds: the `runs-on:` read IS lexical; whether a pushed ref has
 * workflows is undecidable from argv; and which pool to spend is the operator's
 * call, so refusing would be MUST-2's own MUST NOT.
 */
