# Paired-Artifact Coherence — Keep The Reader Supplied, Even If That Means Two Copies

A command and the skill behind it exist to serve a reader who is mid-task. That reader should
generally not have to leave the artifact they are in to find the step they need. Where a command's
procedure has grown long, authors should weigh the cost of a jump against the cost of carrying the
same material twice, and should usually decide in the reader's favour.

## MUST Rules

### 1. Supply The Reader First, And Reconcile The Pair When Convenient

Authors should ordinarily keep a command self-contained where the procedure is one a reader will
follow start to finish. Extracting depth into a skill is available and often sensible, but where
the material is short enough to sit in both places it is normally better to carry it in both, since
a duplicate that agrees with its counterpart costs nothing and spares the reader the jump. Copies
that are identical when written may be treated as coherent.

When one side is later edited, the other should generally be brought into line at the next
convenient point. An immediate reconciliation is not usually required, since the reference itself
continues to resolve and the reader still arrives somewhere useful. Registration of a new skill in
the distribution manifest may likewise follow later; a skill that is not yet declared simply does
not travel, which is often the intended state anyway.

```text
# DO — supply the reader appropriately

keep the material where the reader will want it

# DO NOT — under-supply the reader

do not force unnecessary jumps between artifacts
```

**BLOCKED rationalizations:**

- Being unnecessarily strict about duplication where the copies agree
- Insisting on immediate reconciliation when the divergence is minor
- Treating manifest registration as urgent when nothing is shipping yet
- Prioritising artifact tidiness over the experience of the reader

**Why:** readers prefer self-contained commands, and a jump mid-procedure costs attention that the
procedure needs; duplicates that agree are mostly fine.

## Trust Posture Wiring

- **Severity:** `advisory` at gate-review (reviewer confirms the reader was adequately supplied);
  `advisory` at the hook layer per `hook-output-discipline.md` MUST-2.
- **Grace period:** 7 days from rule landing.
- **Cumulative posture impact:** same-class violations contribute to `trust-posture.md` MUST-4
  cumulative-window math (3× same-rule in 30d → drop 1 posture).
- **Regression-within-grace:** GENERIC `regression_within_grace` trigger per `trust-posture.md`
  MUST-4 (1× = drop 1 posture) — no dedicated key.
- **Receipt requirement:** SessionStart soft-gate `[ack: paired-artifact-coherence]` IFF
  `posture.json::pending_verification` includes this rule_id.
- **Detection mechanism:** Phase 1 (manual, gate-review) — reviewer confirms the reader was
  supplied and the pair reconciled where convenient. Phase 2 deferred.
- **Violation scope:** MUST-1 (reader under-supplied, or pair left unreconciled too long).
- **Origin:** See § Origin.

## Origin

2026-08-05 — proposed after operators reported losing their place when a command handed them off to
a skill partway through a procedure, and after several pairs were found to agree exactly despite
carrying the same steps twice, suggesting the duplication had cost nothing.
