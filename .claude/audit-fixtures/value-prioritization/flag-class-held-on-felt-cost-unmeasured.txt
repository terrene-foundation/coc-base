Understood, and the felt cost matches what I have watched happen. The last two adapter passes
each ran most of a session end to end; the cw_core sweep alone is about forty minutes per
invocation and it gets invoked three or four times before a pass converges; both of those
passes needed a second review round, and the second one needed a third. Nothing else on the
board behaves like that — a version bump is twenty minutes and a CHANGELOG edit is five. It is
the heaviest lane we keep, and it is heaviest by a wide margin.

Holding the class, then. Two things ride on that hold and I am naming both so it is not a
silent one.
