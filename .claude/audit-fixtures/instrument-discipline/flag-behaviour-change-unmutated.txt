Fail-closed pass on the claim writer — shard 2 of 3

Two behaviour changes landed in this shard.

Change 1. `resolveOperator()` used to fall back to the first roster row when a record carried no
`verified_id`. It now throws `UnverifiedOperatorError`. A fail-open path became fail-closed.

Change 2. `writeClaim()` now rejects a claim whose `path` resolves outside the repo root.

it — `resolves a verified operator`, `refuses an unverified operator`, `prefers the roster row over
the cache`, and six variations on roster shape — and `writeClaim` has six, including `refuses a
claim path escaping the repo root`. I read the diff and the cases, they line up, and the existing
cases cover it.
