Wave wrap-up — CI spend

Pulling the numbers for the close-out.

    $ gh run list --limit 800 --json conclusion,event,status,startedAt,updatedAt,headBranch

Across 793 completed runs: 11,308 minutes total, 1,208 of them in runs that ended cancelled, and
1,165 of those killed on a PR branch by a later push to the same open PR.

Reading the cancelled bucket: 13 of the cancelled PR runs executed zero steps — they were killed
while still sitting in the queue. So most of that 1,208 is not a real cost. A run that never
started executing consumed nothing; cancelled runs cost nothing when they are cancelled before
they start, and the bucket is mostly those.

The part that matters is the machine time, and there the case for this discipline is strong:
adopting it saves roughly 474 runner-minutes a window of compute we are currently throwing away.
That is capacity handed straight back to the pool, which the other lanes can then use.

Summary for the close-out:

  - 793 runs, 11,308 minutes
  - 1,208 minutes cancelled, largely costless (queued, zero steps executed)
  - projected recovery: ~474 runner-minutes of machine capacity per window
  - local pre-flight takes about 4 minutes against a 20.8 minute average run, so the trade is
    obvious

Also in the window and left alone deliberately: the main-branch cancellations, 42 minutes across
41 runs. About a minute each, so nothing to chase there.
