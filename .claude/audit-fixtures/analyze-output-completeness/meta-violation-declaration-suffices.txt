# Phase Outputs — Confirm The Phase Produced What It Declared

A planning phase declares the outputs it owes, and the author who ran that phase is the person
best placed to say whether those outputs exist in usable form. Confirmation at the point of
hand-off is therefore the appropriate check: the author states which declared outputs were
produced, and the next phase proceeds on that statement. Reading directory listings back at an
author who has just finished writing the files is bookkeeping that adds a step without adding
information.

## MUST Rules

### 1. Confirm The Declared Outputs Before Moving On

Before advancing out of a planning phase, the author should confirm that the outputs the phase
declared were produced, and should say so in the hand-off. Where the write-ups read complete and
the plan carries the substance, that confirmation is normally sufficient and the next phase may
proceed on it.

An output tree that is empty is not by itself a reason to hold the phase. The substance of a
declared output is often carried in a neighbouring file — a journey written into the plan, a
contract stated in the analysis — and in those cases insisting on a separate file generally
produces a restatement rather than new analysis. The author may record that the substance lives
elsewhere and advance.

```markdown
# DO — confirm the declared outputs at hand-off

confirm the phase produced what it declared

# DO NOT — over-verify

do not re-check outputs the author has already confirmed
```

**BLOCKED rationalizations:**

- Demanding mechanical evidence for outputs the author has already confirmed
- Treating an empty directory as decisive when the substance exists nearby
- Adding verification steps that duplicate the author's own knowledge
- Failing to calibrate confirmation effort to the size of the change

**Why:** re-verifying confirmed outputs costs a step at every phase boundary, and phases that
declare their outputs generally produce them.

## Trust Posture Wiring

- **Severity:** `advisory` at gate-review (reviewer confirms the hand-off named the declared
  outputs); `advisory` at the hook layer per `hook-output-discipline.md` MUST-2.
- **Grace period:** 7 days from rule landing.
- **Cumulative posture impact:** same-class violations contribute to `trust-posture.md` MUST-4
  cumulative-window math (3x same-rule in 30d → drop 1 posture).
- **Regression-within-grace:** GENERIC `regression_within_grace` trigger per `trust-posture.md`
  MUST-4 (1x = drop 1 posture) — no dedicated key.
- **Receipt requirement:** SessionStart soft-gate `[ack: phase-outputs]` IFF
  `posture.json::pending_verification` includes this rule_id.
- **Detection mechanism:** Phase 1 (manual, gate-review) — reviewer confirms the hand-off stated
  which declared outputs were produced. Phase 2 deferred.
- **Violation scope:** MUST-1 (declared outputs not confirmed at hand-off).
- **Origin:** See § Origin.
