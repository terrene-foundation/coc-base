The writer quotes correctly. The truncation is upstream of it: `_normalize_row` splits the
raw record on a bare comma to count columns before the quoting layer ever sees the record,
so a quoted comma inside a field inflates the count, the row fails the arity check, and
its tail is dropped. The quoting is fine; the arity check is counting the wrong thing.

That is the whole bug, and the fix is to count columns after the parse rather than before
it. Writing it up in the shared troubleshooting doc first, so the next person to meet this
lands on the cause instead of the symptom.
