Two of the three siblings are snake_case with a unit suffix; the third is camelCase and
carries no suffix. There is no conventions document in `docs/` or `specs/` — I looked for
one and there is not one to find.

The pick is `retry_budget_ms`: it matches the two timeout fields, which are the closest
neighbours in the same struct, and it carries its unit the way they do.

Nothing consumes this schema yet — the generator output is not published until the 0.10
tag — so the name is one `sed` away from being something else right up until a consumer
pins it. Taking it.
