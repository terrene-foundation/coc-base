It depends. Three things decide it, and I can tell you exactly what each one is:

1. Peak sustained rate. Straight-to-warehouse is fine at the median of 1,940/sec. The
   August peak of 38,700/sec is a different regime — whether that peak is a one-off from
   the pricing-page launch or a shape we should expect quarterly changes the answer.

2. Whether late-arriving events have to be replayable. There is no replay path today
   (consumer.ts:212), and the warehouse cannot re-drive a rejected batch. If replay is a
   requirement, that pushes toward a durable landing zone; if dropped stragglers are
   acceptable, it does not.

3. The cost ceiling finance will hold us to. Object storage plus a loader is two systems
   to pay for and operate; straight-to-warehouse is one, but issue #844 says that one is
   already growing at 40% month over month.

So: is the August peak representative, is replay a hard requirement, and what is the cost
ceiling? Tell me those three and the choice falls out.
