It depends. Three things decide it, and I can tell you exactly what each one is:

1. Peak sustained rate. Straight-to-warehouse is fine at the median of 1,940/sec. The
   August peak of 38,700/sec is a different regime — whether that peak is a one-off from
   the pricing-page launch or a shape we should expect quarterly changes the answer.

2. Whether late-arriving events have to be replayable. There is no replay path today
   (consumer.ts:212), and the warehouse cannot re-drive a rejected batch. If replay is a
   requirement, that pushes toward a durable landing zone; if dropped stragglers are
   acceptable, it does not.

3. The cost ceiling finance will hold us to. Object storage plus a loader is two systems
   to pay for and operate; straight-to-warehouse is one, but issue #844 says that one is
   already growing at 40% month over month.

Here is what I would build under each answer, so you are choosing between plans rather
than between abstractions:

- Peak is a one-off AND replay is not required: straight to the warehouse, with a
  dead-letter table for rejected batches. Smallest system, one bill, and #812 stays open
  by design.
- Peak recurs OR replay is required: land in object storage first, load on a schedule.
  The landing zone absorbs the peak and makes replay a re-read rather than a re-ingest,
  which is what closes #812.
- Cost ceiling is the binding constraint either way: still land in object storage, but
  load into a cheaper columnar table and drop the warehouse's own staging copy — that is
  where the 40% in #844 is going.

Two of the three branches end in the same place, and the evidence I have points at the
branch they share: #812 has been open three months, which is the organisation behaving as
though replay matters, and a pricing-page launch is not a once-ever event. So land in
object storage first and load from there. It costs one more system to run and it is the
slower thing to build — a loader, a schedule, and a backfill runbook, roughly a week more
than the direct path.

I would rather you confirm one fact than have me assume it: is replay a hard requirement?
