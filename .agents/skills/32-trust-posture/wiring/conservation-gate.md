# conservation-gate — governance bookkeeping

**This file is the Trust-Posture Wiring, the rule-graph cross-references and the Origin
record for `.claude/rules/conservation-gate.md`. It is NOT a rule and states no new
obligation — every MUST and MUST NOT token below is a RESTATEMENT of a clause that lives
in the rule, quoted here so the Wiring fields can name what they bind.**

**Why it lives here and not in the rule body.** Claude Code injects every `.md` under
`.claude/rules/` — recursively — as a project instruction, at launch, for every session.
Governance bookkeeping is read by gate-review and by the validators; it is not an
instruction an agent acts on mid-turn. Keeping it in the rule made every session pay for
it. `abridgeV6` already reached this conclusion for the Codex and Gemini lanes, which strip
these exact classes; Claude Code had no equivalent, so it paid in full.

**Why THIS path and not `guides/rule-extracts/`.** Three gates refuse that destination:
`check-descoping.mjs::isInjectedMoveDestination` credits a move only into `.claude/rules/`
or `.agents/skills/` and classes anything else `descoping_to_uninjected` — *"a REMOVAL with
a forwarding address"*; `phase2-deferrals.json` pins verbatim quotes that must keep
resolving; and `check-clause-coverage.mjs` treats a rule with no Wiring heading as NOT
WIRED. A sibling under `.claude/rules/` satisfies the first by construction.

**Why a SUBDIRECTORY and not a flat `*.wiring.md`.** `check-corpus-growth.mjs::listRules`
counts top-level `.md` in `.claude/rules` NON-recursively against a ceiling that sits
exactly at the corpus with no free slot, and `emit.mjs::getCritBaseline` enumerates the
same way. A nested file is therefore neither counted as a new rule nor emitted into the
Codex/Gemini baseline.

**How it is kept out of the session context.** A `claudeMdExcludes` glob. MEASURED with a
bipolar probe on CC 2.1.281: two sibling files, identical in shape, one listed in the glob
and one not — the unlisted one loaded, the listed one did not, while BOTH remained readable
on disk. That last clause is the point: every validator reads this file normally.

## Distinct From / Cross-References

**`instrument-discipline.md` is the nearest neighbour and does NOT overlap.** That rule governs whether a check, ONCE RUN, is evidence — _"would this instrument produce a different result if the proposition were false?"_ THIS rule governs whether a check EXISTS AT ALL for the content that went missing. They compose in one direction only: a conservation enumeration performed with a non-discriminating instrument violates `instrument-discipline.md` MUST-1, while a boundary crossed with NO enumeration violates THIS rule and `instrument-discipline.md` never fires — there is no instrument for it to critique. MUST-2 and MUST-3 here specify WHAT the enumeration must cover (content-identity; the named blind classes); neither restates the discrimination test, which stays that rule's, and MUST-3 is cited BY reference rather than duplicated per `specs-authority.md` Rule 9.

Also distinct from: `evidence-first-claims.md` (claim GRAMMAR — how an assertion is phrased and sourced, not whether a continuity check was run); `sweep-completeness.md` (enumerating outstanding WORK at a session boundary, not content across a transfer); `orphan-detection.md` (a declared artifact that never became reachable — content that never ARRIVED, the mirror of content that DEPARTED); `handoff-completion.md` (whether the receiving side got a usable handoff, not whether the payload was conserved in transit); `sync-completeness.md` (whether every declared target was reached, not whether each target's prior content survived).

**MUST-5 (scratch) cross-references, carried over from the retired `scratch-path-discipline.md`.** Distinct from `rules/state-file-write-guard.md` § residual (k): that clause answers whether a `/tmp` path can WRITE protected state (`out-of-tree` ⇒ no finding); MUST-5 answers where scratch LIVES, and (k) is silent on durability and ownership-by-nobody. MUST-5 does NOT override the corpus's deliberate `/tmp` sandbox examples (`rules/deployment.md`, `rules/build-repo-release-discipline.md`, `rules/worktree-isolation.md`): a sealed, short-lived verify sandbox is a different need from a scratch root a session keeps returning to. It cites, does not restate, the dirty-tree refusal owned by `scripts/ci/dev-preflight.mjs` (pinned at `.claude/test-harness/tests/dev-preflight-receipt-import.test.mjs:1122`). Sibling of `rules/wip-discipline.md` § "Unlanded Work Is Inventory" — both refuse a resting state that quietly costs the work, one on a branch and one under a temp root. MUST-1..4 govern a loss across a boundary a session PERFORMS; MUST-5 governs a loss on a clock NOBODY in the session set — same family (undeclared loss), different owner of the loss.

## Trust Posture Wiring

- **Severity:** `halt-and-report` at gate-review (reviewer at `/implement` + cc-architect at `/codify` confirm that any boundary-crossing operation in the session recorded a before-set, accounted for every departure, tested membership by content, named its instrument's blind classes, and surfaced any empty outcome); `advisory` at the hook layer for the CONTRACT ITSELF per `hook-output-discipline.md` MUST-2 — whether an enumeration was performed and whether its account is adequate are judgments over the session's history with no contract-level structural signal at tool-call time. Per-boundary INSTANTIATIONS are severity-independent of this line and MAY carry `block` where that boundary yields a parsed structural signal (§ MUST NOT, bullet 1).
  - MUST-5 (scratch, folded 2026-10-03 from `scratch-path-discipline.md`): `halt-and-report` at gate-review (cc-architect + reviewer confirm scratch paths in the session's own commands were obtained from the platform rather than spelled, that session-crossing scratch landed in a gitignored durable root, and that nothing whose loss would matter exists only under a temp root); `advisory` at ANY hook layer — there is no structural tool-call-time signal for it: the discriminator is a lifetime and ownership question about a path's TARGET, so a lexical `/tmp` scan is a token-presence check on a semantic property, BLOCKED as a verdict by `rules/instrument-discipline.md` § MUST NOT and barred from `severity: block` by `hook-output-discipline.md` MUST-2/MUST-5.
- **Grace period:** 7 days from rule landing (2026-08-16 → 2026-08-23).
  - MUST-5 keeps the grace it landed with as a standalone rule: 2026-10-01 → 2026-10-08.
- **Cumulative posture impact:** same-class violations (a boundary crossed with no recorded before-set; a departure left undeclared; a membership decision taken on names/shas/path-presence; an enumeration reported whole-set with an unnamed blind class; an empty outcome reported in the grammar of a completed one, including going quiet after a refusal) contribute to `trust-posture.md` MUST-4 cumulative-window math (3× same-rule in 30d → drop 1 posture; 5× total in 30d → drop 1 posture).
  - MUST-5 same-class violations: a scratch root hand-spelled instead of obtained from the platform; an artifact whose loss would matter left only under a temp root; a later-session handoff left as a standalone temp snapshot; session-crossing scratch left untracked in the tracked tree.
- **Regression-within-grace:** a same-class violation within the 7-day grace window routes through the GENERIC `regression_within_grace` emergency trigger per `trust-posture.md` MUST-4 (1× = drop 1 posture) — NO dedicated per-clause trigger key.
